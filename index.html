How discoveries work

Each cleared level brings one basic ingredient. Bonus ingredient chance: 25%, guaranteed after three clears without a bonus. Recipe scrap chance: 5%, guaranteed after eight clears without a scrap. Two scraps reveal a recipe. Missing ingredients are favored for bonus drops. Discoveries save immediately, even if the run ends. Once all recipes are found, scraps become stamps. First bakes earn 3 stamps; stamps buy pantry ingredients. Rewards are cosmetic and do not change timed campaign gameplay.
